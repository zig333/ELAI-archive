## 📖 What is ELAI-archive?

ELAI-archive is a **research archive** — a carefully organized collection of experiments, code, and ideas about building **AI agents** that can help you with coding tasks. Think of AI agents as "digital helpers" that understand language and can perform actions for you.

This project explores **local-first** technology, meaning it focuses on running AI on your own computer rather than relying on cloud services. It's **model-agnostic**, which means it doesn't care which AI brain (like GPT, Llama, or others) you decide to use.

The archive is **abandoned and unsupported**, so it's perfect for **curious learners, researchers, and hobbyists** who want to explore how AI agents work under the hood.
